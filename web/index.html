Q-systems: a graph of every alternative, rewritten

A Q-system does not rewrite a string: it rewrites a graph of strings, arrows carrying trees between an entry and an exit, every path one string. A rule a1 + … + ap == b1 + … + bq. never replaces anything: for every path its left side matches, it adds a path beside it, and goes on until no rule applies to a path it has not been applied to. Only then are the rewritten arrows removed. Under -INV- the same rules run backwards. Step through a phase, or edit it: qsys, compiled to WebAssembly, runs in this page.

What is reconstructed, and from what

The machine follows Colmerauer's report, Les systèmes-Q ou un formalisme pour analyser et synthétiser des phrases sur ordinateur (publication interne n° 43, Université de Montréal, September 1970), which gives the whole grammar, the meaning of every construct, and the printed results of its examples. Every rule, the page it rests on, and every place a decision was made, is in docs/decisions.md.

Every computation the report gives a result for comes out as it says: the AAABBBCCC system arrow by arrow through its first phase, and its inverse; $$; structured strings; the cul-de-sac; and the paraphrase treatment, whose analysis gives the tree of p. 38 and whose synthesis gives the graph of pp. 38-40 line for line, vertex numbers and all. The eighteen sentences of pp. 40-41 come out in the reverse of the report's order.

What reconstructing it found is in docs/research.md: the tree-to-string rules of p. 30 do not converge as printed (open structure); removing one string removes every string that shares an arrow with it (e07-shared-arrows); and an ambiguous sentence comes out of one analysis with both its readings, as two arrows between the same two vertices (e05-two-readings).
